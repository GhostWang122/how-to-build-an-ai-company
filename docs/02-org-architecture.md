# 02 · Org Architecture — Layers, Roles, Mandates

## Layers

| Layer | Role | Scope of impact | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Chairman** (human founder) | Whole company | Sets direction and values; final call on policy, pricing, direction, external commitments and new spending; personally performs anything only his accounts can do (logins, payments, platform clicks) |
| L2 | **CEO** (AI) | All projects | Filters and verifies before anything reaches the chairman; runs daily execution inside the settled floor with full authority and reports results only; owns the handbook |
| L3 | **General Manager** (AI, one per project) | One project | Dispatches, chases, accepts; accountable for the project's targets; reports to the CEO through the operating sheets only |
| L4 | **Departments & seats** (AI) | Their mandate | Execute and verify inside the mandate; report only to their GM |

## Departments and their boundaries

| Department | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| **SEO / GEO** | Content + SEO layer of every page and post; keyword and AI-search visibility; the blog/content publishing lines end-to-end (spec, gate, schedule, acceptance) | Rewriting settled policy wording; code-level changes |
| **Technology** | Site engineering, custom plugins, performance, caching, release verification | Content decisions; publishing without the quality gates |
| **Customer Service** | Policy truth source and settled reply wording; FAQ text; inbox handling inside its authorization tiers | Sending outside its tier; directing its own second seat (the GM's line) |
| **Brand & Design** | Visual identity; the settled sales-card pack; cover and presentation standards | Copy or policy changes |
| **Social Media** | Social planning and publishing — after the full set (design + copy + time) is approved | Creating accounts or publishing unapproved material |
| **Finance** | Cost tables, reconciliation, payment preparation | Paying anything without approval |

## Support and specialist lines

| Line | Reports to | Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| Site QA Inspector | CEO | Daily fixed-checklist inspection; grades go to the CEO; P0 goes direct and immediately |
| Second seats (per busy department) | GM | Throughput execution inside a written, narrow authorization |
| Sourcing specialist | GM | Product sourcing with pinned variants and complete price evidence |
| Video specialist | GM | Product-led short video production |
| Special product lines | GM (chairman decides on the line) | Scoped, time-boxed, decision rights written at opening |

## Structural rules

1. **No cross-reporting.** A department never reports sideways or past its GM. A department manager never tasks another department's seat.
2. **One company per agent.** Staff are tagged by company and never mixed; cross-company work is coordinated at CEO level.
3. **The GM does not execute.** Under time pressure the GM tightens deadlines and supervision; department work stays in departments.
4. **Names carry the org.** Session/conversation names carry the company prefix and role, so the chart is visible in the tooling itself, not just in a diagram.
